About Alpha Hill Medical Centre

Pioneering comprehensive, integrated and affordable healthcare for every family in Nandi County.

Who We Are

A pioneering healthcare facility in Mosoriot

Alpha Hill Medical Centre exists to make comprehensive, integrated and affordable healthcare available to every family in our region — without compromising on quality or compassion.

We always take care to ensure we comply with KMPDC and Ministry of Health standards. This also means consistent compliance with our medical ethics, which is the basis of all we do. We are pioneers and role models in care standards.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To preserve life and enhance health by enabling access to comprehensive, integrated, high-quality and most affordable care — delivered with kindness, integrity and respect.

Our Vision

Alpha Hill Medical Centre will continue to provide a continuum of high-quality, cost-effective services in order to improve the health status of the community we serve.

Why Your Voice Matters

Feedback that shapes better care

At Alpha Hill Medical Centre, we are committed to delivering high-quality, compassionate healthcare. Listening to the people we serve is one of the most important ways we improve.

Whether you want to celebrate a member of our team, tell us what went well, or point out something we can do better, your honest feedback helps us raise our standard of service for you and the whole Nandi County community.

  • Your feedback goes directly to our care team.
  • We use it to improve our services and patient experience.
  • For urgent medical concerns, please call us instead.

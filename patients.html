Before You Arrive

What to Bring on Your Visit

  • National ID or passport
  • Insurance card / SHA details (if applicable)
  • Any previous medical records, prescriptions or referral letters
  • A list of your current medications

Our emergency, laboratory and pharmacy services are open 24 hours a day, every day of the year. No referral is required for outpatient consultations.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Are you open on weekends and holidays?

Yes. Our emergency, laboratory and pharmacy services operate 24 hours a day, every day of the year.

Do I need a referral to see a specialist?

No referral is required for outpatient consultations. Walk in or call ahead to book.

Do you accept SHA?

Yes — we are SHA-accredited, alongside AAR, MUA, M-TIBA and First Assurance. See our Insurance page for details.

How do I pay if I don't have insurance?

We offer affordable cash rates for all services, in line with our mission of accessible care.

Where are you located?

Along Mosoriot – Kabiyet Road, Mosoriot, Nandi County. See our Contact page for directions and a map.

Still have a question?

Our team is glad to help you, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

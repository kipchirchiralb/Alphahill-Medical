Welcome to Alpha Hill

Modern healthcare, delivered with kindness

Alpha Hill Medical Centre is a modern hospital along the Mosoriot – Kabiyet Road in Nandi County, offering high-quality, affordable healthcare to the community we serve.

Guided by kindness, our expert team provides ICU care, dialysis, major surgeries, physiotherapy and comprehensive maternity services — supported by advanced diagnostics including CT scans, X-rays, endoscopy, ultrasound, optometry and dental care.

We are accredited by SHA, KMPDC, NCK and PPB, so you can trust that every service meets national standards of quality and safety.

Health Awareness & Community

Caring beyond our walls

Safe Motherhood

Attend antenatal clinics and choose skilled delivery care — the surest way to protect both mother and baby.

Early Screening Saves Lives

Regular checks — from eye exams to endoscopy — help detect conditions early, when they are easiest to treat.

Know Your Numbers

Keep track of your blood pressure, blood sugar and weight. Simple, routine checks are the foundation of lifelong health.

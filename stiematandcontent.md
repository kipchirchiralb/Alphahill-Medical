### Page Title: Our Medical Services

## 3.1 Intensive Care Unit (ICU) Services

Our Intensive Care Unit is a specialised facility equipped with advanced life-support systems and monitored by a highly trained critical care team. We provide 24/7 continuous monitoring and treatment for critically ill or unstable patients — because in critical moments, every second and every detail counts.

**Highlights:**
- Advanced life-support and monitoring systems
- Dedicated critical care team on duty 24/7
- Continuous patient monitoring and rapid intervention

## 3.2 Maternity Services

We offer quality care for mother and baby at a friendly price. A healthy baby born safely of a healthy mother is our joy and pride. Our maternity team provides exceptional normal delivery and caesarean section (CS) delivery services in a safe, supportive environment.

**Highlights:**
- Skilled normal and CS deliveries
- Antenatal and postnatal care
- Mother-and-baby friendly wards

## 3.3 Major & Minor Surgeries

Our surgical department provides comprehensive procedures, plaster application, splinting and traction. Our theatre capabilities include general surgeries, orthopaedic surgeries, gynaecological procedures, ENT surgeries and urological surgeries — all performed by experienced surgical teams.

**Highlights:**
- General, orthopaedic, gynaecological, ENT and urological surgery
- Fracture management: plaster, splinting and traction
- Modern, fully equipped operating theatre

## 3.4 CT Scan Services

We offer state-of-the-art Computed Tomography (CT) scanning for rapid, highly detailed cross-sectional imaging of the body — providing critical diagnostic insights for trauma, internal injuries, vascular conditions and complex diseases.

**Highlights:**
- Fast, detailed cross-sectional imaging
- Essential for trauma and emergency diagnosis
- Interpreted by qualified specialists

## 3.5 X-Ray Services

We X-ray both bone and soft tissue. Our accurate X-ray services cover the skull, chest, lower limb, upper limb, abdomen, spine, pelvis and shoulder for complete diagnostic imaging.

**Highlights:**
- Full-body coverage: skull to lower limb
- Bone and soft-tissue imaging
- Quick turnaround for results

## 3.6 Ultrasound Services

Welcome for an OBS/GYN ultrasound done with the latest technology. Our precise ultrasound services include obstetric, pelvic, abdominal, breast, Doppler, thyroid and scrotal scans.

**Highlights:**
- Obstetric and gynaecological scans
- Abdominal, breast, Doppler, thyroid and scrotal scans
- Latest ultrasound technology

## 3.7 Endoscopy & Colonoscopy

We provide endoscopy and colonoscopy to examine the digestive tract, colon and rectum. These diagnostic procedures help our medical team detect polyps, bleeding, inflammation and early signs of colorectal cancer — early detection saves lives.

**Highlights:**
- Diagnostic endoscopy and colonoscopy
- Early detection of colorectal cancer
- Performed by experienced specialists

## 3.8 Dental Services

Our dental department is readily available to keep you smiling with strong teeth. Every dental procedure is done at a reasonable rate, including extraction, filling and root canal treatment.

**Highlights:**
- Extractions, fillings and root canal treatment
- Affordable, transparent pricing
- Friendly, gentle dental team

## 3.9 Eyecare / Optometry

We protect and care about your eyes. Don't suffer in silence — get a computerised eye checkup at our special clinic to detect early signs of glaucoma, macular degeneration and diabetes-related eye conditions.

**Highlights:**
- Computerised eye examinations
- Screening for glaucoma, macular degeneration and diabetic eye disease
- Specialist eye clinic

## 3.10 Dialysis Services

We provide renal dialysis for patients with kidney conditions, delivered by trained staff in a safe, comfortable environment — bringing life-sustaining care closer to the Nandi community.

**Highlights:**
- Modern dialysis unit
- Trained renal care staff
- Affordable, accessible sessions

## 3.11 Physiotherapy

Our physiotherapy department helps patients regain movement, strength and independence after injury, surgery or illness through personalised rehabilitation programmes.

**Highlights:**
- Post-surgery and injury rehabilitation
- Personalised treatment plans
- Qualified physiotherapists

## 3.12 24-Hour Laboratory Services

Our laboratory operates round the clock, providing accurate and timely test results that support fast diagnosis and treatment — day or night.

## 3.13 24-Hour Pharmacy

Our fully stocked pharmacy is open 24 hours, ensuring you never have to wait for essential medication. Licensed by the Pharmacy and Poisons Board (PPB).

## 3.14 Inpatient & Outpatient Care

Whether you need admission for close monitoring or a same-day consultation, our inpatient wards and outpatient clinics provide attentive, respectful care for every patient.
